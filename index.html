The footnotes are the point: “beginning 9/4”, “NO LCM 9/11 & 9/18”, “closing at 1pm beginning 9/12”, “through 9/6 only” — each one is a date on which the schedule stops being what the paragraph above it says. Live

Tap Lap Swim, Swim Team or Synchro in the filter to see just that. The arrows move between weeks, and each block says how many lanes it holds. On a phone the same schedule opens as a list.

What this replaces

  • A schedule published twice. The typed hours and the poster say the same thing in two places that have to be edited together.
  • Seven footnotes that are really dates. Long Course begins 9/4, except not on 9/11 and 9/18; Saturday closes at 1pm from 9/12 and at 12pm from 10/10; Sundays end on 9/13. In the calendar those are just the weeks in which the blocks differ — look at Friday and Saturday of this week.
  • Both closures are in it. Labor Day is an empty Monday; the private swim team event on Saturday 9–11am is a booking like any other.
  • “A minimum of 6 lanes available” stops being a promise in prose — every block says how many lanes it holds, so a swimmer can count.
  • No “sign up for our email updates to receive monthly lane availability updates” needed: the page is the update.
